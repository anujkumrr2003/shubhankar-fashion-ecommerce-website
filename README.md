# Shubhankar Fashion – Global B2B & D2C E-commerce Platform

**Main Website (D2C):** https://shubhankarfashion.com/

**B2B Website (India):** https://shubhankarfashion.com/in/

## Project Overview

Shubhankar Fashion is a global fashion e-commerce platform that combines B2B (Business to Business), B2C (Business to Consumer), and D2C (Direct to Consumer) business models in one integrated online shopping ecosystem.

The platform is designed to serve both individual customers and business buyers, providing different shopping experiences, pricing structures, and international shopping support.

It supports customers across multiple countries with country selection and local currency price display.

## Business Models

### 1. B2B – Business to Business

**Website:** https://shubhankarfashion.com/in/

A dedicated India-focused storefront for business buyers, including retailers, salons, clinics, and other businesses.

**Key Features:**
- Dedicated B2B shopping experience.
- Business-oriented product pricing.
- Bulk purchase pricing slabs.
- GST-inclusive invoicing.
- Business customer support.
- Product categories and collections.
- Bestseller product listings.
- Shopping cart and checkout.

### 2. D2C – Direct to Consumer

**Website:** https://shubhankarfashion.com/

A direct-to-customer online shopping experience that allows customers to browse and purchase fashion products directly.

**Key Features:**
- Direct online shopping for individual customers.
- Product categories and collections.
- Bestseller products.
- Product browsing and details.
- Retail shopping experience.
- Shopping cart and checkout.
- Customer support and FAQs.
- Shipping and return information.

### 3. B2C – Business to Consumer

The platform also supports individual retail customers through its consumer shopping experience, offering product discovery and online purchasing.

## Global Shopping & Currency Conversion

## Installation & Setup

1. Install XAMPP or another compatible PHP and MySQL environment.
2. Place the project folder inside the web server directory.
3. Start Apache and MySQL.
4. Create the required MySQL database.
5. Import the clean database schema, if provided.
6. Configure the local database connection.
7. Open the project through localhost.

## Security

- Do not publish production database credentials.
- Do not include private customer information.
- Keep API keys and payment gateway secrets outside the public repository.
- Use a separate local configuration for development.
